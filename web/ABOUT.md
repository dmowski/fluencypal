# How to Get the Most from AI English Practice with FluencyPal

FluencyPal is an AI-powered platform for practicing spoken English through conversation, role play, vocabulary training, grammar review, stories, debates, and community features. It is designed to help learners improve speaking confidence, fluency, grammar, and vocabulary through active practice.

## Learning Plan: Start with a Smart Survey

When you begin using FluencyPal, you will be invited to complete a guided survey. This is not a marketing questionnaire about your age or hobbies. It is a real learning tool that helps build your personal study plan.

The most important part is to describe your goal honestly. You should explain what you want to achieve, where you feel weak, and where you already feel confident. The first question may be something like: **“Tell us about your goal.”**

Based on your answer, the AI creates additional follow-up questions. These questions are designed to understand your situation in more detail and build a learning plan that matches your needs.

## Just Talk Mode: Practice Speaking with AI

In FluencyPal, you can simply talk with AI and practice real communication. There are several speaking modes.

### Call Mode

In Call Mode, you see an AI avatar that speaks to you. You reply naturally, and you can also choose your own conversation topic. If you get stuck, FluencyPal can show prompts to help you continue. You can also read the transcript during the conversation, which makes it easier to follow what the AI said.

### Voice Message Mode

This mode is calmer and more structured than live calling. You see the transcript and record your answer as a voice message. After you record it, the system shows what can be improved before you send it.

The flow is simple:

1. You see and hear the AI message.
2. When you are ready, you record your reply.
3. You press **Done**.
4. FluencyPal analyzes your response and suggests improvements.
5. You can re-record your message or send it as it is.
6. The AI then responds and the conversation continues.

This mode is useful if you want to focus on the quality and accuracy of each response.

### Chat Mode

Chat Mode is similar to Voice Message Mode, but instead of recording your answer, you type it. This is useful when you cannot speak out loud or want a quiet way to practice.

### Adjustable AI Speaking Speed

You can adjust the AI voice speed to make it more fluent or slower and calmer. This helps you choose a pace that matches your listening level.

### Speaking Practice with Ongoing Improvement

During conversations, the AI remembers the areas where you need improvement. It then uses this information in a separate practice section focused on your real mistakes.

Overall, **Just Talk Mode** helps you practice speaking naturally while also improving grammar, fluency, and confidence in a gentle way.

## Personalized Grammar Rules

After each conversation, FluencyPal creates a personalized list of grammar rules and explanations based on your mistakes.

Each rule appears as a separate item. When you open it, you see:

- a simple explanation of the mistake
- examples related to the rule
- a short quiz to help you practice it

The quiz helps you build correct sentences and reinforce weak areas in your grammar. In addition to the quiz, you can also practice the rule with an AI teacher.

The AI teacher guides the conversation toward that specific grammar topic, so you practice the rule in live communication instead of only reading about it.

This means that FluencyPal helps you close grammar gaps through three steps:

- simple explanation
- interactive quiz
- live speaking practice with AI

## Word Practice Based on Your Learning Plan

This speaking mode is focused on vocabulary growth.

Based on your goal, FluencyPal creates a list of useful words that can help you reach that goal. At the start of each vocabulary lesson, the system selects 10 words that are relevant to your current level, your goal, and your existing vocabulary.

The AI teacher explains each word and then practices it with you through conversation. You do not just memorize words. You use them in context during a dialogue.

At the same time, FluencyPal continues updating your personalized grammar suggestions in the background.

## Role Play Based on Your Learning Plan

FluencyPal creates role plays based on your personal goal.

For example, if your goal is to find a job in English, your role plays may include:

- screening interview with a recruiter
- technical interview
- salary discussion
- job-specific interview situations

These role plays are designed to help you practice realistic conversations that match your real-life goals.

## Rules Practice Based on Your Learning Plan

FluencyPal also builds lessons around grammar and language patterns that are important for your goal.

If your goal requires formal communication, job interviews, travel conversations, or everyday life situations, the system can prioritize the rules that are most useful in those contexts.

This makes grammar practice more practical and goal-oriented.

## Learning Plan: Continuous Refinement

Your learning plan is not fixed. As you continue using FluencyPal, the system makes notes about what should be improved next.

Your plan is updated over time, so future lessons can better reflect your real progress, weaknesses, and needs.

## General Role Plays for Everyday Practice

In addition to goal-based role plays, FluencyPal includes a library of ready-made role plays for general speaking practice.

These are useful when you want something simple and practical. Examples may include:

- speaking in a shop
- talking to technical support on the phone
- everyday service conversations
- interactive language games like Alias

There are around 20 ready-made practice modes in total.

## Custom Role Plays: Create Your Own Scenario

If you want a more specific situation, you can create your own role play.

The **Custom Role Play** feature allows you to write your own conditions for the scenario. This gives you more control and lets you build a practice session that matches your exact needs.

## Stories: Interactive Reading and Quiz Practice

FluencyPal includes a feature where you can listen to stories and complete quizzes based on them.

While listening, you can see:

- the original text
- the translation

In the quiz, you rebuild the text word by word. This helps you get used to sentence structure in the language you are learning.

It can be a useful alternative to passive scrolling through short videos. For example, you can use Stories while commuting to work.

## Daily Question: Practice Expressing Your Ideas

FluencyPal offers a daily question feature where you answer one new question every day.

You can also see answers from other users. This helps you practice expressing opinions and building responses to interesting or challenging topics.

## Debates: Speak with Other People in a Comfortable Format

FluencyPal also allows users to challenge each other to debates.

The process works like this:

1. You invite another person to a debate.
2. The other person accepts.
3. Both participants receive questions.
4. Each person records their answers.
5. The answers are saved in the system.
6. After all answers are submitted, the system chooses a winner.

The platform also explains why one participant won and awards points to the winner.

This creates a structured and less stressful way to practice speaking with other people.

## Game Mode: Improve Vocabulary, Speaking, and Reading

FluencyPal includes a game mode with different quiz activities, such as:

- translating words
- building sentences
- reading text
- discussing questions

You earn points for correct answers. The top 5 users on the leaderboard receive full access as long as they remain in the top 5.

This adds motivation and makes practice more engaging.

## Community: Ask Questions and Share Progress

FluencyPal includes community chats where users can:

- ask questions
- share progress
- discuss learning challenges
- support each other

Age verification may be required for some community features.

## Leaderboard and Gamified Motivation

FluencyPal includes a leaderboard system. The more you use the platform, the more points you earn.

Points can be earned through:

- game mode
- Stories quizzes
- Daily Questions
- community participation
- conversations with AI

The top 5 users on the leaderboard receive full access for as long as they stay in the top 5.

## Why FluencyPal Is Different

FluencyPal is not just a chatbot for English learners. It combines:

- AI speaking practice
- personalized grammar support
- vocabulary lessons based on your goal
- realistic role plays
- interactive stories
- daily speaking prompts
- debates with other learners
- gamified progress and leaderboard rewards

This makes FluencyPal useful for learners who want more than passive study. It is built for active speaking practice, targeted improvement, and long-term progress.
