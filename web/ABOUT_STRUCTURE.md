Here’s a clean SEO-friendly URL structure for FluencyPal.

I would keep it under one clear section like **`/features/`**. That makes the site easier for users, search engines, and AI systems to understand.

## Recommended URL structure

### 1. Main overview page

**URL:** `/features/`
**Title:** FluencyPal Features: AI English Speaking Practice, Grammar, Role Plays
**Meta description:** Explore FluencyPal features for AI English speaking practice, personalized grammar, vocabulary lessons, role plays, stories, debates, and community learning.

### 2. Learning plan

**URL:** `/features/learning-plan/`
**Title:** Personalized Learning Plan for English Practice | FluencyPal
**Meta description:** Build a personalized English learning plan with AI. FluencyPal creates lessons and practice based on your goals, strengths, and weak areas.

### 3. AI speaking modes

**URL:** `/features/ai-speaking-practice/`
**Title:** AI Speaking Practice for English Learners | FluencyPal
**Meta description:** Practice speaking English with AI through live calls, voice messages, and chat. Improve fluency, confidence, and communication skills.

### 4. Personalized grammar rules

**URL:** `/features/personalized-grammar-rules/`
**Title:** Personalized English Grammar Practice with AI | FluencyPal
**Meta description:** Get personalized grammar rules based on your mistakes. Practice English grammar with explanations, quizzes, and AI conversation.

### 5. Vocabulary practice

**URL:** `/features/vocabulary-practice/`
**Title:** AI Vocabulary Practice for Real Conversations | FluencyPal
**Meta description:** Learn useful English words based on your goals. Practice vocabulary in context with AI lessons and guided conversation.

### 6. Goal-based role plays

**URL:** `/features/role-play/`
**Title:** English Role Play Practice with AI | FluencyPal
**Meta description:** Practice real-life English conversations with AI role plays. Prepare for job interviews, daily situations, and goal-based speaking tasks.

### 7. Custom role plays

**URL:** `/features/custom-role-play/`
**Title:** Create Custom English Role Plays with AI | FluencyPal
**Meta description:** Build your own English role play scenario with AI. Practice speaking in situations that match your personal or professional needs.

### 8. Rules practice based on goals

**URL:** `/features/goal-based-grammar-practice/`
**Title:** Goal-Based English Grammar Practice | FluencyPal
**Meta description:** Practice grammar that matches your learning goals. FluencyPal focuses on the English rules most useful for your real conversations.

### 9. Interactive stories

**URL:** `/features/interactive-stories/`
**Title:** Interactive English Stories with Quiz Practice | FluencyPal
**Meta description:** Improve reading, listening, and sentence structure with interactive English stories, translations, and word-by-word quizzes.

### 10. Daily questions

**URL:** `/features/daily-questions/`
**Title:** Daily English Speaking Questions | FluencyPal
**Meta description:** Practice answering a new English question every day. Build confidence, express ideas, and improve speaking with daily prompts.

### 11. Debates

**URL:** `/features/debates/`
**Title:** English Debate Practice with Other Learners | FluencyPal
**Meta description:** Practice spoken English through structured debates. Record answers, compare arguments, and improve speaking in a guided format.

### 12. Game mode

**URL:** `/features/language-games/`
**Title:** English Learning Games for Vocabulary and Speaking | FluencyPal
**Meta description:** Improve English through interactive games for vocabulary, sentence building, reading, and speaking while earning points.

### 13. Community

**URL:** `/features/community/`
**Title:** English Learning Community and Progress Sharing | FluencyPal
**Meta description:** Join the FluencyPal community to ask questions, share progress, discuss learning challenges, and stay motivated.

### 14. Leaderboard

**URL:** `/features/leaderboard/`
**Title:** English Learning Leaderboard and Rewards | FluencyPal
**Meta description:** Earn points for practice and climb the FluencyPal leaderboard. Top users unlock rewards through active English learning.

## Small SEO improvements

I’d slightly simplify some naming for search intent:

- use **“AI speaking practice”** instead of “Just Talk”
- use **“role play”** instead of internal names
- use **“interactive stories”** instead of just “Stories”
- use **“language games”** instead of “Game”
- use **“personalized grammar”** because that phrase is clearer for SEO and AI parsing
